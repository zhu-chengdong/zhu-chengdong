# Chengdong Zhu · 朱铖栋

PhD researcher in Electronic Engineering at the **University of York**, working on underwater robotics, robot perception, and localization and mapping.

My work spans robot platform design and integration, sensor calibration and experimental data collection, and SLAM methods and evaluation. I am also beginning work on agricultural robotics, with a focus on navigation and robotic arm applications.

[Website](https://zhuchengdong.com) · [Email](mailto:chengdong.zhu@york.ac.uk) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5877-2650)

## Research and projects

- **Underwater robot platform development** — Design and integration of the ROV V1 platform, including mechanical design, electrical and sensor integration, ROS integration, and experimental testing.
- **[York-Tank](https://github.com/zhu-chengdong/York-Tank)** — Underwater data collection, stereo calibration and rectification, and dataset development for visual SLAM under degraded imaging conditions. The dataset is [publicly available on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23347574).
- **Underwater localization and mapping** — Image preprocessing and enhancement, feature extraction and matching, SLAM development, and evaluation of localization and mapping performance.
- **Agricultural robotics · ongoing** — Robot system integration and debugging, SLAM, path planning, and robotic arm control for crop-health monitoring and agricultural applications.
